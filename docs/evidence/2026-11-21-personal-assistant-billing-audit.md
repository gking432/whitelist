## Existing Stripe enrollment is an agency plan

[Partner checkout](../../app/join/actions.ts), [billing client](../../lib/billing/stripe.ts), [webhook](../../app/api/billing/stripe/route.ts), [partner enrollment SQL](../../supabase/migrations/20260904150000_partner_self_service.sql) and [capacity SQL](../../supabase/migrations/20260904182000_paid_client_capacity.sql) implement a partner/agency flow:

- It is configuration-gated and validates the selected recurring/setup price objects before checkout. The current `PARTNER_V1_PLAN` source constants are USD $500 monthly, $1,000 setup and ten managed-client slots. Those are existing agency constants, not proposed personal-assistant prices or verified live Stripe configuration.
- Checkout has a durable enrollment key/provider idempotency key. A signed completion webhook checks payment/subscription data and invokes the service-only fulfillment routine, whose enrollment-row lock protects the resulting partner/membership creation and replay.
- Subscription changes read current provider state before updating enrollment/onboarding billing status. The capacity trigger locks enrollment to gate adding managed-client slots; its comments explicitly preserve existing managed workspaces and normal edits.
- The owner billing portal is selected by the existing enrollment's Stripe customer. This is not a per-business workspace subscription, included AI dollar allowance, prepaid excess-credit ledger, or universal AI stop when an agency subscription changes.

The first-owner business path and imported Stripe invoices/payments must not be treated as this enrollment. Source-app money records describe the customer's business; they are not our platform subscription or permission to charge for AI. There is no new checkout, plan, price, charge, metering webhook or commercial activation in this increment.

## Proposed policy for later implementation, not an active entitlement

The desired commercial direction is a monthly workstation subscription with an explicitly stated included AI allowance, visible warnings and a hard stop on new paid model work at the approved limit. Prices, tiers, application-count limits and warning thresholds remain hypotheses pending measured costs and owner approval. No prices or product tiers are selected here.

Before paid personal-assistant operation, a separately reviewed implementation needs:

1. A durable workspace entitlement and billing-period/price version, separate from both legacy agency enrollment and the owner's external personal-assistant plan. Include model input/output/cache/tool costs and other applicable paid services rather than equating all tokens with one price.
2. An atomic reservation at every paid backend entry, child/delegated task and permitted retry. Reserve against workspace, team, run/tree and platform caps in a consistent lock order; enforce bounded fan-out, depth, iteration, time and retry limits. Concurrency must not oversubscribe included allowance or approved excess credit.
3. A conservative unknown-outcome policy, idempotent settlement and reconciliation tied to the actual attempt/receipt. Do not release an unknown reservation as free usage, silently retry a lost response, or hide accepted cost because output was withheld.
4. Extra spend only through an explicit prepaid or capped owner-approved amount and period. No automatic uncapped overage or top-up; a general business approval is not extra AI-spend consent. Stops apply to new paid calls while source reads, history, manual controls and existing action reconciliation remain available.
5. Fail-closed behavior when entitlement, price/cost evidence, reservation or accounting is missing/unavailable, with an honest paused-cost state and a recoverable owner decision. Verify warnings, race/retry/restart behavior and hard stops with pure/isolated tests before any authorized paid acceptance.

An external assistant connection does not transfer its subscription, identity, memory or app credentials to our backend. [Official OpenAI pricing guidance](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing) distinguishes API-key pricing from ChatGPT included usage. This repository does not verify any separately claimed API-credit benefit or subscription-sharing entitlement. Do not promise that an existing personal-assistant plan funds workstation backend calls, or that our controls govern unrelated actions outside the workstation.

## Non-model integration contract delivered

[integration-contract.ts](../../lib/personal-assistant/integration-contract.ts) is a strict, pure future boundary with no retrieval endpoint, grant creation, model call or execution method. It separates verified workstation access from verified in-workstation conversation, labels our own assistant separately from an existing personal assistant/shortcut, and disallows self-approval. Status carries tenant-scoped record revisions, observation clocks and explicit freshness/coverage; absent quote/team/history/reply evidence remains absent. Planned, pending approval, approved future work, pending/uncertain provider outcomes and sent receipts are distinct. A sent claim requires matching human approval and a succeeded external receipt. This structural check cannot authenticate a caller or prove the referenced records exist; a future adapter must use the current authorized canonical customer/team/work/approval/receipt read models.

Only the new pure contract tests were run. Their structural values are not a seeded customer quote/follow-up history or provider integration proof. Runtime assistant connectivity/conversation, cost enforcement, paid usage and commercial acceptance remain unimplemented/unverified by this audit.
