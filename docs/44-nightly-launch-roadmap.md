# Roadmap to the complete business operating app

Updated November 5, 2026 after correcting the earlier roadmap. This is the product build order for the existing application. Hosting, database recovery and Google setup are one foundation task. They are not the full product roadmap.

**November 16 execution correction:** use the [parallel development brief](45-parallel-build-brief.md) to build the product and [build progress](46-build-progress.md) to resume it. Hosted account access is a live verification/release gate, not a global prerequisite for source development. A coordinator and bounded workers should implement shared context, enforced owner authority, manager controls, onboarding, observation and the remaining workflows while blocked account checks stay explicitly pending.

## The finished product

**Latest CRM implementation priority — owner decision:** finish this money checkpoint, then implement HubSpot through one customer-owned authorized account per business, reusing the current adapter. Missing accounts need an explicit owner setup/create-account step. Keep source attribution and customer consent, separate tenants, and charge for our own solution rather than claiming resale rights to HubSpot. The [current developer terms](https://legal.hubspot.com/hs-developer-terms) and [remote MCP documentation](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/apps/developer-platform/build-apps/integrate-with-the-remote-hubspot-mcp-server) support planning a documented scoped integration; no live test or agreement approval has occurred. Do not pool businesses in one account or promise invisible provisioning. HighLevel is technically capable; fit for the precise managed hidden-backend arrangement is unconfirmed, not a finding that it is broken/prohibited. Its terms include white-label reseller qualifications and standalone-feature MAP exclusions, so do not assume a broad competitor/MAP restriction conclusively applies to this CRM-only arrangement.

The current bounded CRM package implements customer-owned HubSpot OAuth with exact account/scope verification, encrypted CAS refresh, contacts/deals/notes read models and reviewed deliberate contact/note creation through the provider gateway. Legacy inferred upserts are disabled; token read probes report read evidence only. Source/isolated acceptance status is tracked in document46. Controlled contact/deal field updates remain the next acceptance target: explicit field ownership and approved changes, latest-version comparisons, readback/reconciliation and a stated read/write race limitation. The current source-app handoff is a package boundary, not a permanent inability of agents to update CRM records. No real-contact migration, activation, paid subscription or hosted change is authorized by this decision.

**November 17 owner correction — authoritative CRM:** a business’s supported external CRM owns its customer/deal/pipeline data. A business without one should use a proven external CRM wired through this app; the first implementation priority is an authorized customer-owned HubSpot account per business. Assess exact API/scopes, tenant isolation and account setup before integration; invisible provisioning, account pooling and CRM resale are not supported claims. Our local stores may retain identity mappings, source-backed read models and orchestration state, but must not become a competing writable CRM. Calendar, payment and task applications retain their separate responsibilities. This supersedes homegrown-CRM fallback language in earlier documents and does not authorize deleting current tables or changing hosted data.

The migration dependency is a provider-backed customer/relationship gateway: map stable tenant/provider/account/object identities to current local references; route authoritative customer/deal changes through supported adapters; keep authority, assignments, owner review, approvals/outbox and receipts local; reconcile version/conflict/deletion/permission behavior; and cut callers over incrementally with contract and real isolated database tests. Current `lib/crm/internal.ts`, `lib/agents/runner.ts`, department work and connector projections still depend on local `crm_*` writes. Existing HubSpot/GoHighLevel catalog entries and adapters are partial integration evidence, not proof of the complete authoritative mode or automatic provisioning. Source-linked invoice review tasks in this wave are orchestration, and do not create or adopt CRM customers.

Research recorded November 17 is advisory. HighLevel is a strong default candidate because its [official MCP](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/other/mcp/index.html) supports scoped agent operations and its [subaccount API](https://marketplace.gohighlevel.com/docs/ghl/locations/create-location/) documents per-business creation on the Agency Pro/Founder tier. Its [Founder plan page](https://help.gohighlevel.com/support/solutions/articles/48001180534-introducing-the-highlevel-founder-plan) lists a $497 monthly base; this is not a $497 per-client price, and usage/add-ons require separate assessment. HubSpot and Attio also offer official read/write MCP capabilities; no unique “most agent-friendly” or segment market-share winner has been established. HubSpot remains a plausible first bring-your-own connector. A hidden-backend/custom-app arrangement needs commercial confirmation against the intended agreement and [HighLevel terms](https://www.gohighlevel.com/terms-of-service), including relevant resale/MAP, competitive-use and white-label provisions; these notes are not a conclusive legal interpretation or unrestricted-rights claim. Technical documentation is not live acceptance.

Proposed future onboarding asks neutrally how actively the business uses its current CRM. Low visible activity does not establish non-use, especially if integrations write records. With explicit owner approval, evaluate a managed default and preview supported migration: bounded read-only evidence, import preview/deduplication, retained source identities and consent/opt-out, original-data retention and verified cutover. Do not silently migrate, enroll workflows, merge contacts from claimed addresses, or delete/cancel old CRM accounts. No vendor purchase/provisioning or migration implementation is authorized by this research update.


An owner signs up, describes their business and connects the applications they already use. The product prepares a business-context draft and recommends relevant workflows and agent responsibilities. The owner reviews the setup and chooses what can run, what needs approval and what stays off.

The owner sees one calm workspace: what matters today, conversations, work to finish, money due, progress toward goals and decisions requiring them. They can ask the business manager questions or change instructions in chat. Agents and ordinary automations share the same internal customer and business records, coordinate work across connected applications, wait for human decisions when required, perform supported actions and verify the results.

Over time, the system learns from connected activity and the owner's explanations, identifies the actual handoffs behind a workflow and proposes ways to remove work. Improvements are measured against evidence. The owner does not have to maintain or even open the CRM. The phone experience is simple; desktop provides more detail. Both use the same operating system and records.

The initial packaged version serves owner-operated appointment businesses. The architecture supports additional business playbooks and specialist modules over time. Custom enterprise implementation and eventual white-label distribution remain separate expansion tracks; completing the app does not require rebuilding those companies' software or the partner/platform CRMs.

## What already exists

Reuse the existing app, not a new project:

- Responsive Today, Messages, To-do and Manager screens, with an optional detailed CRM.
- Shared customer/business records, durable agent assignments, role definitions, delegation, approvals and action queues.
- Existing website, email, SMS, Instagram and scheduling paths, plus connector adapters with different levels of implementation.
- A new read-only Google connection/import path with account-scoped email/calendar views and connection controls.

These pieces have substantial local verification, but they do not establish a complete live product. On November 5 the hosted database was unavailable; real Google authorization and continuous hosted sync remained unverified. The most recent local connected-data verification passed 324 unit tests, a production build and a database journey with mocked Google responses. There are no iOS or Android application projects yet.

Use [Connected data](43-connected-data.md) and [Shared customer team](39-shared-customer-team.md) for the actual implementation evidence and limits. A feature is not complete merely because a role, prompt, adapter or screen exists.

## Ordered product tasks

These are meaningful product work packages. Each can contain several nightly builds. We select a working increment from the active package each night, with an observable completion check; we do not count every configuration screen as a separate product task or promise the whole app in eighteen nights.

## The dependency order

Run task 1 as the hosted/live-validation track while independent implementation proceeds. Do not wait for Google consent or the hosted database to implement and contract-test the other work. Tasks 2–5 create the connected operating context and owner surface; tasks 6–8 provide reliable coordinated work and control; tasks 9–12 deliver the business jobs; tasks 13–15 make the product adaptive and repeatable; tasks 16–18 finish distribution and release. The coordinator in document 45 resolves actual code dependencies and integrates parallel work. Required real database/provider checks must pass before the affected capability is accepted for production.

This is not a requirement to build every conceivable connector before using the agents. Prove an inquiry-to-approved-booking journey with the first supported stack as soon as its dependencies work, then extend each category through the same contracts. Begin observation and outcome measurement during those trials so improvement claims have a baseline. Exercise the phone experience throughout; native packaging does not require waiting for every optional module.

Hosting, security, provider reviews and recovery work continue alongside features. External approval delays can overlap independent work. An owner's first useful live workflow can be available well before all eighteen work packages are complete.

## Personal assistant onboarding foundation; live integration remains planned

**November 21 owner requirement, clarified:** include a small personal-assistant launcher at the bottom right of the workstation. The intended interface is the personal assistant the owner already uses (examples: Muse, ChatGPT Dot and Grok Bot), talking to the background business-agent team through our workstation. We give that assistant scoped access to our business context, managers, work status and action proposals; this is not a request to borrow the assistant's existing Gmail/CRM credentials. Our own assistant is the fallback for users without a compatible personal-agent connection, with its identity clearly labelled. Actual in-workstation conversation with the user's existing assistant is a separate provider integration requirement. The owner’s subsequent November21 instruction authorizes one bounded onboarding foundation: optional Yes/No/Skip preference, private actor/business persistence, setup/status surface, non-model contracts and a billing audit. It does not authorize a live connector, provider partnership, model call or continuing roadmap build.

- Reuse the existing manager-chat, assistant-console and floating-panel foundations. Coordinate with the live-call panel and phone navigation so controls do not overlap or produce competing assistant windows. The current live-call overlay is not itself the requested general personal assistant.
- Keep personal conversations private to the signed-in person and explicitly distinguish them from shared business-team context. Passing personal context or tasks into business work must be clear and authorized; account/workspace switching and revocation must preserve that boundary.
- Treat the personal assistant as the owner's conversational coordinator, while business managers and specialists continue their persistent background work. A question about a quote can consult the CRM, email, SMS or social manager as relevant, using the same customer identity and shared work history. Return source-backed status, completed effects, pending decisions, next planned actions and escalation rules; do not invent a history to make the answer sound coordinated. Read-only status questions do not need a new approval for each internal lookup within the granted scope.
- Preserve a clear boundary between querying status, asking a manager to investigate, preparing a new action, approving it and executing it. The assistant can ask the team for an update or prepare a proposal; actual consequential actions still require the owner's approval. Share only the authorized business context needed by the request, not the personal agent's unrelated private memory.
- Distinguish an embedded assistant we operate, an actual connection to the user's existing personal agent, and an external-app shortcut. Calling a provider's model API does not establish access to that user's agent identity, memory, history, subscription or existing app connections. A shortcut must not be labelled as a completed in-workstation integration.
- The primary integration direction is an approved connector, MCP server or supported agent protocol exposing our workstation to the user's personal assistant. Background business workers use the workstation's separately authorized app connections. No copying another assistant's credentials is required. Exposing our tools to an outside agent and displaying that same agent's conversation inside our UI must each be verified; success in one direction does not establish the other.
- All consequential business work initiated through the assistant must use the workstation's current permissions, owner approval, execution and receipt/reconciliation controls. An external agent cannot approve its own proposals. Do not claim our controls govern unrelated actions performed outside the workstation under another product's permissions.
- Use provider names/icons only as allowed by their branding and integration terms, identify the actual active assistant and connection status, and retain a functional default when an optional provider cannot connect. Do not promise universal compatibility.

Feasibility evidence checked November 21: [Muse connector guidelines](https://platform.muse.ai/docs) and [Poppy agent/business protocol](https://muse.ai/blog/poppy-personal-agent-protocol) describe exposing business capabilities to personal agents; [Grok connectors](https://docs.x.ai/grok/connectors) document custom MCP connections. For ChatGPT Dot, [official dots guidance](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/enterprise/dots-admin-guide) describes using the owner's allowed ChatGPT app connections, and [custom MCP plugins](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/custom-mcp-server) provide a route to expose our workstation tools, subject to account/workspace permissions. This is an integration route to validate, not proof that our connector is implemented. [Dot messaging channels](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/channels) document ways to reach the same dot; a generic external embedded-chat API was not verified. Do not substitute an API-powered model for the user's personal Dot identity or claim that a connector alone embeds its chat.

Owner-provided illustrative acceptance scenario: “What is the status of that quote? I have not heard anything.” The personal assistant asks the relevant managers and reports the actual quote/send history, owner-approved email follow-ups, the next proposed or approved SMS and any planned owner-call escalation. Each sent action must have its real approval/execution evidence; “planned,” “awaiting approval,” “approved for tomorrow” and “sent” must remain distinct. A claim of no reply includes the latest checked source/freshness, and replies, opt-outs or changed source state suppress stale next steps. This is a target scenario, not an existing customer history or completed feature.

Acceptance: demonstrate that status question against the real scoped team and records, including partial/missing information and a proposed next action entering the existing approval flow. Then prove one existing-personal-agent connection with the owner's account, truthful agent identity, explicit data scope, actual manager responses and disconnect/revocation. Verify the in-workstation chat channel separately through an official provider-supported interface; a model substitution, logo, external launch link or connector used only in the assistant's native app does not satisfy that UI requirement. Our own clearly labelled assistant can supply the same workstation workflow as a fallback. Test desktop/phone layout, private/shared visibility and approval enforcement. No new provider account, credential, paid call, publication or production change is authorized. Complete and verify this one preference/setup package, callback the supervising chat and stop for owner review. Recurring supervision remains off; remaining integration work needs a fresh owner instruction.

**Bounded foundation being implemented:** onboarding asks “Do you use a personal AI assistant?” with Yes, No and Skip, plus explicit Muse, ChatGPT Dot, Grok, another-assistant or clearly labelled workstation-assistant preference as appropriate. The selection is private to the actual signed-in person and selected business, reversible through setup/settings, and never a credential or connection success. A bottom-right text-only setup/status panel shows the saved choice and truthful unavailable/setup-needed state. No working chat composer, invented replies/history, unsupported iframe or external shortcut is substituted for an actual integration. No personal conversation is passed into shared business-manager context. Account/business changes and revoked access must not reuse a stale page’s private state.

**Voice is excluded for now.** This package does not enable or extend voice, audio, Realtime or paid voice paths. Existing voice code and earlier optional-future module descriptions do not establish current voice scope or acceptance.

**AI usage and billing policy — proposed, not activated:** use a monthly workspace subscription with an explicit included AI allowance, visible warnings and a hard stop on new paid work at the approved limit. Extra use requires an explicit prepaid top-up or approved capped policy, with no surprise automatic overage. Every backend, child task and permitted retry needs concurrency-safe reserve-before-call and reconciliation, separate workspace/run/team/platform caps and loop limits, and fail-closed accounting. Read-only non-model status/history should remain usable and should not call paid managers gratuitously. A personal assistant’s provider plan is distinct from workstation backend costs. The existing daily token ceiling is not a dollar/subscription cap, and existing partner Stripe enrollment is not this product billing. See the [source audit and implementation gaps](evidence/2026-11-21-personal-assistant-billing-audit.md). No new dollar cap, entitlement, price, product or charge is claimed implemented.

Pricing levels and connected-app limits remain hypotheses pending measured service/workflow costs. A possible premium marketing hub for owner-approved Google/Meta campaigns and A/B tests remains a future concept, with separate explicit ad-spend budgets and current approval/execution controls. The owner’s immediate priority is the connected single-pane application; tiers, advertising integrations and campaign execution are outside this package. Next core live acceptance remains genuine sign-in, working authorized connections, persistent shared data/background sync and one actual proposal → owner approval → supported provider result/receipt shown in the workstation. Isolated fixtures do not establish those live gates.

## Specialist modules and later expansion

The complete core supports adding modules; it does not turn every business into a purchaser, reseller or call center.

- **Retail/reselling:** inventory, market-value research, listing/fulfillment work and margin-backed sourcing proposals, using explicitly supported marketplaces and retained evidence.
- **Purchasing:** supplier comparison, approved purchase requests, order/receipt tracking and exceptions for businesses that need them. Do not enable spending or negotiation through a prompt alone.
- **Voice:** optional inbound answering, routing and supported booking/customer handoffs.
- **Additional programs:** extra booking, CRM, accounting, social, commerce and task systems through the shared connector contracts. API integrations are preferred where suitable; browser/computer operation is a separately permissioned supported implementation, not a promise that logging into any app makes it reliable.

Custom enterprise work adds discovery and bespoke workflows inside a client's existing programs, potentially with a department-specific single pane. White-label rights, partner packaging and partner/platform CRMs follow the proven product; they are not prerequisites for its core operation. The broader [business capability inventory](37-business-capabilities-and-business-types.md) remains the extension catalog.

## Nightly execution

Latest owner sequencing is functional connected application → simple modern adaptable desktop/phone UI → coordinated agents → later marketing/advertising departments. The build and supervisor exchange assignments/completion callbacks after tested GitHub checkpoints, with a cumulative handoff ledger in document46. The supervisor manages one heartbeat; a completion callback is not a promise that this chat keeps running after its turn. For UI personalization use explicit preferences and evidenced use with stable navigation and pin/reorder/reset controls. Later campaign agents may prepare detailed plans and creative from reviewed business knowledge; spending/publication still requires saved bounded authority/budget and verified supported receipts.

Each night choose an increment that produces a working capability, not just another mock screen. Record the business outcome, code/configuration changes, verification and the next unfinished increment. Infrastructure/access steps are substeps of task 1, not separate roadmap milestones.

Examples of appropriately scoped nightly increments after access is available:

- A real Google connection keeps the owner's email and schedule updated in the hosted app while the browser is closed.
- A real customer inquiry becomes a scheduling assignment and a phone approval, followed by one confirmed calendar event and customer confirmation.
- The manager's saved “stop Instagram” instruction blocks new work and cancels the relevant unsent work, with proof visible to the owner.
- An approved follow-up is sent once and is automatically suppressed if the lead has already booked or opted out.
- The observer shows one evidenced workflow and a measurable proposed improvement instead of an unsupported general recommendation.

Some of these increments require multiple sessions. Split them when needed without relabeling every small setup step as another completed product capability.
